# Mission Reflection

## Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, Azure impressed me the most. I found it easier to understand while doing the research, especially because it has different services for computing, storage, databases, and other cloud needs. I also liked that it works well with Microsoft technologies.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many things in common. All three provide cloud services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and security. They also allow users to choose resources based on what they need and scale them when necessary.

Business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform. A company should look at its budget, the type of applications it uses, storage needs, security, and the number of users. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may find Azure more suitable, while a company focused on AI may prefer Google Cloud.

Being a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not just about picking the most popular one. I learned that I need to look at the actual needs of the client first. Comparing the services of AWS, Azure, and GCP also helped me understand that similar services can have different features and strengths.

After completing this mission, I think my GitHub portfolio became more organized and useful. I added information about the three cloud platforms, comparison tables, client recommendations, and screenshots of my activities. I also became more comfortable creating Markdown files and organizing my work in GitHub. Overall, this activity helped me understand cloud platforms better and gave me more experience in documenting technical work.
